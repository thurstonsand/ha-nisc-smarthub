# NiteFlex tariff and bill facts

## Question

What are the exact published terms of Cobb EMC's NiteFlex rate schedule, which bill components sit outside the published rates, and what does the account's first two weeks of usage say about which terms bind?

## Resolution

[`research/niteflex-tariff.md`](../research/niteflex-tariff.md) transcribes the 2026 rate schedule and records the bill-time unknowns (PCA rider with its Super Off-Peak exemption, sales tax, Operation Round Up) plus the usage split observed over the first 13 days. The 400 kWh allowance projects to roughly half used per cycle, so cycle-boundary error is zero dollars today; On-Peak carries 46% of usage at the highest rate.
